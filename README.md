# 🐰 Cute Rabbits Resource Pack

Cute Rabbits Resource Pack enhances the rabbits in Minecraft by giving them a softer, more adorable appearance while keeping the original feel of the game. Each rabbit has been carefully redesigned with smoother textures, gentle pastel color palettes, and more expressive, charming faces. The goal of this pack is to make your world feel warmer, calmer, and more visually pleasing without breaking immersion.

Every variant has been uniquely styled, from light cream tones to soft browns and clean whites, ensuring diversity while maintaining consistency with Minecraft’s natural environment. Whether you're building peaceful gardens, cozy cottages, or aesthetic survival worlds, these rabbits will blend in beautifully and enhance the overall atmosphere.

This resource pack is designed for players who enjoy relaxing visuals, detailed textures, and a more wholesome experience. It fits perfectly with nature-based builds, cottagecore themes, and anyone looking to add a subtle but meaningful visual upgrade to their gameplay.
